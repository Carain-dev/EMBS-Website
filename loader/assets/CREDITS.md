**Human heart for Cycles** by **elZancudo** — licensed under

Originally published on BlendSwap (#73924); obtained from the public mirror

Changes made for this site: converted from OBJ to glTF/GLB, Meshopt-compressed,
a simplified mobile level of detail, textures re-encoded as WebP, venous blue
tones graded toward a muted warm tone, tissue micro-relief normal maps derived
from the colour textures, and a pre-rendered fallback still (`heart-still.webp`).
