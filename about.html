Who We Are

Engineering in Medicine & Biology Society

The IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS) Student Chapter at KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology is a student-driven community dedicated to advancing biomedical engineering through research, innovation, and collaboration. We bridge the gap between technology and healthcare.

Our Vision

To be a leading student chapter that inspires the next generation of biomedical engineers and healthcare innovators.

Our Mission

To foster interdisciplinary learning, research, and community engagement at the intersection of engineering and medicine.

Research & Innovation

Key Focus Areas

01

Biomedical Signal Processing

Analysis and interpretation of physiological signals such as ECG, EEG, and EMG for clinical and research applications.

02

Medical Imaging

Exploration of imaging modalities including MRI, CT, and ultrasound, with emphasis on image enhancement and diagnostics.

03

AI in Healthcare

Application of machine learning and deep learning models to assist in disease detection, prediction, and personalized medicine.

04

Wearable Health Technology

Design and development of wearable sensors and embedded systems for continuous health monitoring and remote patient care.

Our Journey

Chapter Timeline

2018

Chapter Founded

The IEEE EMBS Student Chapter was officially established at KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology, marking the beginning of a student-driven biomedical community.

2020

First Hackathon

Organized our first MedTech Hackathon, bringing together students to solve real-world healthcare challenges using engineering and technology.

2022

Regional Recognition

Received recognition at the IEEE Regional level for outstanding chapter activities, research contributions, and student engagement.

2024

Growth Milestone

Crossed 340+ active members, launched the EMBS Podcast, and expanded research collaborations across multiple departments.
