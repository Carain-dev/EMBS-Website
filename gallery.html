Our Journey

Chapter Milestones

2025

Launched the EMBS Innovation Lab, hosted the first inter-college AI in Healthcare Hackathon, and crossed 400 active members.

2024

Received IEEE Regional Best Chapter Award, launched the EMBS Podcast, and published three student research papers in biomedical journals.

2023

Organized the MedTech Symposium with 300+ attendees, established industry partnerships, and introduced the annual project showcase.

2022

Expanded chapter activities with weekly workshops on signal processing, wearable technology, and embedded systems for healthcare.
