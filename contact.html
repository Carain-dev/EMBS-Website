FAQ

How do I become an IEEE EMBS member?
To join IEEE EMBS, first create an account at ieee.org and register as a student member. Then reach out to us at ieeeembs@kpriet.ac.in to be added to our chapter. Membership gives you access to IEEE publications, priority event registration, and our chapter community.
Who can attend EMBS events?
All students of our college are welcome. Some events are also open to participants from other institutions.
How do I submit an achievement or blog post?
Send your achievement details or blog draft to ieeeembs@kpriet.ac.in with the subject line "Achievement Submission" or "Blog Submission". Our editorial team will review and publish it on the website. We welcome research highlights, event reports, tutorials, and student project write-ups.
How can I be a guest on the EMBS Podcast?
We welcome students, faculty, researchers, and industry professionals as podcast guests. Write to us at podcast.embs@kpriet.ac.in with a brief introduction and the topic you'd like to discuss. Our podcast team will get back to you to schedule a recording session.
