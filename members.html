Become an IEEE EMBS Member

Join a thriving community of biomedical engineering students and researchers. As an IEEE EMBS member, you gain access to cutting-edge research resources, hands-on workshops, international conferences, networking opportunities, and a global network of engineers and healthcare innovators dedicated to advancing medicine through technology.

  • Access to IEEE digital library and publications
  • Priority registration for workshops and hackathons
  • Mentorship from faculty advisors and industry experts
  • Official IEEE membership certificate and student ID
