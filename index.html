Why Join IEEE EMBS?

Leadership

Take on real roles in organizing events, managing teams, and representing IEEE EMBS at regional and national levels.

Research

Collaborate on biomedical research projects, publish findings, and gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge medical technologies.

Networking

Connect with students, faculty, and industry professionals across the global IEEE EMBS community and build lasting relationships.

Podcast & Media

Be part of our student-run podcast, create content, and amplify the voice of biomedical engineering to a wider audience.
